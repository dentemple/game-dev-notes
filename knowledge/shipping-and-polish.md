# Shipping & Polish (Launch Checklist)

## Mindset (primary)

"Launching a game is a project in itself. Give it the same planning and effort you gave the development." The gap between feature-complete and successfully launched is where indies succeed or fail.
Source: https://www.strayspark.studio/blog/ue5-indie-game-release-checklist (accessed 2026-11-18).

## Localization (primary)

Minimum coverage: English (base), Simplified Chinese, Japanese, Korean, German, French, Spanish (Latin American), Brazilian Portuguese, Russian. "UI and menu localization is essential. Full dialogue localization can come post-launch if budget is limited."
Source: https://www.strayspark.studio/blog/ue5-indie-game-release-checklist (accessed 2026-11-18).

## Store page (primary)

- Capsule images: 460x215, 231x87, 616x353, 374x448, 600x900; header 460x215.
- Screenshots: minimum 5, aim 8-10 (1920x1080 minimum).
- Trailer: 60-90 seconds, gameplay first, story second.
Source: https://www.strayspark.studio/blog/ue5-indie-game-release-checklist (accessed 2026-11-18).

## Wishlist benchmarks (primary)

- ~10,000 wishlists → modest success; 30,000 → strong launch week; 100,000+ → potential breakout.
- First-week conversion typically 15-20%. Fewer than 5,000 wishlists → delay launch and invest in marketing.
Source: https://www.strayspark.studio/blog/ue5-indie-game-release-checklist (accessed 2026-11-18).

## Timeline (primary)

Store page live + wishlisting ~12 months out → Next Fest demo ~6 months → QA/marketing ramp ~3 months → final testing ~1 month → launch → +1 month stability/community → +3 months first content update.
Source: https://www.strayspark.studio/blog/ue5-indie-game-release-checklist (accessed 2026-11-18).

## Note for solo dev

Start the store page and localization months early; the "last 20%" is a parallel project (localization + store + certification + marketing converge at once).
