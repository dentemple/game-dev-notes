# Market & Consumer Expectations

## Steam wishlist benchmarks (data aggregator — see caveat)

- Median launch-week wishlist→sale conversion: **12.8%**.
- Year-one unit sales average **0.58x** of pre-launch wishlist count.
- Games launching under 5,000 wishlists: **82% probability of under $10,000 gross revenue**.
- Steam Next Fest participation: average net **+4,350 wishlists**.
Source: https://voxbooster.com/blog/steam-wishlist-conversion-statistics-2026/ (accessed 2026-11-18).

## Caveat (important)

Steam revenue medians are contested: "games that sold almost nothing leave little owner-count or review trace, so they fall out of the denominator" — the sample skews toward winners. Treat any "median indie revenue" figure as optimistic.
Source: https://steamforecast.app/reports/steam-wishlist-benchmarks-reality-check (accessed 2026-11-18).

## Quality bar (practitioner/press)

- "the quality of certain indie games blurs the gap between them and top-end AAA titles" — photorealistic lighting, physics, and cinematic animation are now accessible to solo devs, raising the entry floor.
- First impressions (storefront icon/screenshot, then the first minutes) drive success; players judge visuals in under a second, but "you cannot screenshot game feel."
Source: https://exputer.com/features/indie-games-feel-aaa-2026/ (accessed 2026-11-18).

## Survivorship bias (practitioner)

Schedule One, Hollow Knight, and Stardew are "extreme outliers, not representative outcomes." Don't model decisions on the "rare 0.1%" — scope 1-2 month projects as a beginner, at most ~6-12 months once experienced.
Source: https://daily.dev/posts/be-very-aware-of-survivorship-bias--oxg75sqqx (accessed 2026-11-18).

## Note for solo dev

Plan around median outcomes, not breakout hits. A focused core loop plus a premium first impression beats a bloated 100-hour game; respect the player's time.
